Jihwan Kim

Physical oceanographer · Tokyo, Japan

I work as an Ocean Engineer at CLS, where I develop oceanographic analyses and forecasts for tuna fisheries. My research focuses on how ocean circulation and climate variability affect fish distribution and catches.

Previously, I worked with the North Pacific Fisheries Commission on fisheries research and vessel-monitoring data. I received my Ph.D. in Physical Oceanography from Seoul National University in 2023.

Research & applied work

Climate and tuna catch forecasts

CLS / fisheries research

I use statistical and machine-learning models to study the relationship between ocean conditions and tuna catches. ENSO and Indian Ocean Dipole signals support climate-informed catch forecasts up to 6 months ahead.

My North Pacific research also includes stochastic forecasting of Pacific saury distribution and physically consistent super-resolution using a multi-head attention U-Net.

Daily fishing-area maps

CLS / CATSAT

I develop automated Favorability Maps using sea-surface temperature, chlorophyll, currents, ocean fronts, and weather. These maps support fishing-area selection and voyage planning for more than 10 commercial clients and approximately 150 tuna vessels in the Pacific, Indian, and Atlantic Oceans.

Fisheries monitoring data

North Pacific Fisheries Commission

At NPFC, I processed and quality-controlled approximately 9 million vessel-monitoring records over three years. I developed algorithms linking VMS and AIS data with transshipment declarations and inspection reports, identifying over 100 data inconsistencies and anomalous cases annually.

I also built GIS tools for comparing Pacific saury, chub mackerel, and neon flying squid catch-and-effort data across member submissions.

Korea/Tsushima Strait observations

Seoul National University / 2020–2023

I designed a replacement observation system, analysis method, and cloud data server to restore submarine-cable voltage monitoring after equipment failure. The measurements are used to estimate volume transport through the Korea/Tsushima Strait.
